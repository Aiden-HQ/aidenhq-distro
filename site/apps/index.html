From a sentence to a live app

Four steps, one of them yours.

You make one early choice and one final tap. The agents do everything in between, and each step is checked before the next.

  1. Ask

    "Build a roadmap tracker grouped by quarter." On Telegram, Slack, the board or a doc comment.

  2. Pick a look

    The UI Designer drafts a few layouts. You pick one; that is the only design question you get.

  3. Built and checked

    An Engineer builds it, automatic checks run, a preview goes up, and a second AI reviews the change.

  4. Live

    The DevOps Engineer deploys it. Your team signs in with your company login.

Kept running

Apps that look after themselves.

Most AI-built apps are demos that rot. Yours are watched around the clock. When something breaks, agents roll it back, find the cause, fix it and ship the fix after review, often before anyone notices.

  • Health checks on every app, with automatic rollback of a bad release.
  • Fixes, not just alerts. An Engineer opens a fix with a test for the failing case.
  • Kept up to date. Dependencies and known security holes are patched as reviewed changes.
  • Visual checks. A UI Reviewer compares screenshots of every change before it ships.

Built for real use

Real apps, on your data, for your team.

Apps run on your own server, next to your agents and your data, and they look and feel like one product.

  • Share with colleagues

    Invite people by email. They sign in with your company login and see only the apps you share. Each app's admins manage who has access.

  • Built on your data

    Every tool you connect feeds one shared database. An app gets its own private space in it and reads only what it is allowed to.

  • A preview for every change

    Each change gets its own preview link, so you can click through it before it goes live.

  • Pinned to your board

    Any app can become a panel on your board, so the views you asked for sit next to your tasks.

  • One design system

    Apps start from a shared starter and design system, so they look consistent and polished from day one.

  • Yours to keep

    The app starter and design system are MIT licensed, so the apps your agents build carry no licence strings.

How it stays safe

Each app runs in its own locked box with no access to the rest of your server unless you grant it. Passwords and keys reach an app as files, never inside its code. Every deploy is an approval checked by a second AI, and opening an app to more people is your decision. More on safety
