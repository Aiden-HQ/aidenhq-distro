Get started

From one line to talking with Aiden.

Your AI assistant installs AidenHQ and shows its progress as it goes. Then you open one link and take three quick steps.

  1. 1. Install with your AI

    Paste this into Claude Code, Codex or any coding assistant. It asks for a server (or uses this machine) and your email, then does the rest.

  2. 2. Open your link

    When the install reaches 100% your assistant gives you one setup link. Open it, then create your password and authenticator and connect your model key.

  3. 3. Three quick steps

    Save your recovery key, connect Telegram, connect Slack. Each takes a minute, and the board explains it as you go.

  4. 4. Talk to Aiden

    Aiden says hello, learns how you work and takes your first job. Everything after this happens in conversation.

On a Mac, keep it awake and on power so your agents keep working. Prefer a plain command? See every way to install.
