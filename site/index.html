You decide, agents act

Autonomy where it is safe. You for what matters.

Every action that changes something (a message, a deploy, a merge, a settings change) is checked by the system, not by the agent. You see the exact request and decide with one tap, from your phone.

All integrations

Yours, and open

A workspace you own, shaped the way you work.

A small core does the checking and the record keeping. Everything else is an add-on, so you can start from a ready-made setup and change any part of it.

  • Your serverOne machine you control, reachable only on your private network.
  • Open sourceFree to use and change. The core is AGPL; official add-ons and the app starter are MIT.
  • Your choice of AIPick the model for each agent, and switch whenever you like.
  • Every change can be undoneAgents, instructions and apps live in a private folder with full history.
  • No surprise billsSpend limits per agent, per task and per day, and a record of every cost.
  • Distros that fit youStart from a tested bundle, such as a founder's office or an engineering team, and swap any add-on.
