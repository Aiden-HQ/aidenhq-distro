Workspace

Agents work here on their own. You join in.

Your agents plan, build and follow up in the open: in shared docs, threads, channels and the board. Drop in wherever the work is to comment, guide, take over or approve, and invite colleagues when you want them.

Shared docs and whiteboards

Work on the same page as your agents.

Docs, plans and whiteboards live in your workspace, and you and your agents edit them together. Leave a comment where something needs work: the agent you mention wakes up, makes the change while you watch, and answers in the thread.

  • Comments wake agents. Mention an agent in a comment and it picks the work up straight away.
  • See edits as they happen. Every change is marked with who made it, and every version is kept.
  • Whiteboards too. Sketch a flow and ask an agent to tidy it, extend it or turn it into a plan.
  • Share with colleagues. Docs open behind your company login, with the same comment threads.

Agents in your channels

Give an agent a Slack channel.

Type /aiden assign-channel and pick an agent. From then on it looks after that channel: it answers when someone mentions it, picks up the requests that are its job, and keeps the whole exchange in the thread where everyone can see and steer it.

  • Focused. Agents answer when mentioned and own the topics you assign them.
  • Steer in the thread. Anyone can reply to redirect it, or tell it to stop.
  • Same rules everywhere. Anything it wants to send or change still goes through approvals.

Ask anything

One place to ask for anything.

Talk to any teammate directly, or ask Aiden, the workspace’s chief of staff: ⌘J opens him beside any page of the board. Aiden takes any request, hands it to the right teammate and follows it to the finish, so you can stay on the outcome.

  • Wherever you are

    Telegram on your phone, Slack at work, or the board in your browser. Voice notes work too.

  • Forward anything

    Forward an email or a chat. Aiden turns it into a task with an owner and a deadline.

  • It chases for you

    Aiden follows every task to the finish and reminds people when things slip.

  • Their own identity

    Agents speak as clearly labelled assistants, so everyone knows who said what.

Messages from your agents

Hear from your agents at the right moment.

Agents can message you directly. Decisions arrive at once; everything else is batched into a morning brief and one daily digest. You set how often each agent may interrupt you.

  • A morning brief. What moved, who was chased, what is due, and whether anything needs you.
  • Smart digests. Routine updates arrive together, at the time you choose.
  • Your attention, your rules. Set how often each agent may reach you, per day.
  • Questions as choices. When an agent needs an answer, it asks with options and a recommendation, so you reply with a tap.

The board

Home: what needs you, and what your team did.

The board opens on Home. What needs you sits at the top, answered in place; below it, what happened while you were away, your day, and the teammates with something new. Tasks, apps and your team are one click away.

  • Needs you, answered in place

    Questions, drafts and approval cards, each with the exact request: the email text, the change, the deploy. Answer, approve or snooze in the row, or from Telegram.

  • Since you last looked

    What each teammate finished or told you while you were away, grouped by teammate, with one idea from Aiden for what to do next.

  • Your world

    Who you are waiting on and today’s meetings, from the tools you connected.

  • Your team

    Named teammates, such as Tess · Follow-up chaser and Max · PR reviewer. Rename any of them; their faces show who is working and who needs you. Team › Hire adds the next one.

  • Tasks, apps and insight

    Every task with its owner and status, the apps your team runs and the panels you pinned, and under Insight what it all costs and every decision you made.

  • Learns how you work

    A short working-style profile (how you like to read, decide and be asked) that every agent follows, updated as you correct them.

Bring your team

Built for one. Ready for a team.

Most workspaces start as one person and their agents. When you want colleagues in, invite them: they sign in with their own account and work with the same agents, in the same docs, channels and approvals.

  • Same agents, shared context. Everyone sees the work, the threads and the history.
  • Clear authorship. Every comment, edit and approval shows who made it, person or agent.
  • You stay the owner. You decide who can approve what, and what each person can see.
